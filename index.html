# Research

I'm interested in the principles of self-supervised representation learning for image and video foundation models, aiming to construct latent spaces that robustly encode semantics and spatiotemporal dynamics. I leverage the geometry of the resulting feature manifold to analyze data structure and propagate sparse relational supervision. Some papers are highlighted.

ReCoCo: From Patches to Compositions for Visual Representation Learning
Juan Gutiérrez, Saman Zadtootaghaj, José Luis Blanco-Murillo, Sara Atito
arXiv, 2026
arXiv | bib

Region-level self-supervision over compositions of prototype predictions, for stronger dense features.

Invertible Transport Between Sample-Only Distributions via Projected Characteristic Functions
Juan Gutiérrez, Víctor Gutiérrez-García, José Luis Blanco-Murillo
Preprint, 2026

Invertible flows toward priors known only through samples, by matching projected characteristic functions.

PANC: Prior-Aware Normalized Cut for Training-Free In-Context Segmentation
